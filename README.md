# Penalty Kick Shooter

A playable soccer penalty kick experience developed as part of my YinzCam application. The project focuses on intuitive controls, skill-based gameplay, and polished presentation.

## Gameplay & Design

The goal was to make a simple mechanic engaging and rewarding to master.

- **Aim & Shoot:** Click to aim at a target, then hold to build shot power.
- **Perfect Shot:** Time your release to hit the perfect shot zone, rewarding precision and skill.
- **Dynamic Presentation:** Cinemachine camera shots, a title screen, team branding, and audio feedback create a cohesive experience.

## Development Approach

I began with a simple blockout to validate the core interaction, camera framing, and gameplay feel before moving on to visual polish.

Once the core mechanics were working, I integrated stadium and player assets, customized the player uniform textures to match the team's blue, and added sound effects to bring the experience to life.

Gameplay logic was kept independent of the animation implementation, allowing the underlying systems to evolve without requiring a rewrite of the core mechanics.

## Built With

- **Engine:** Unity 6 (6000.0.58f2)
- **Camera System:** Cinemachine
- **3D Assets:** Third-party stadium and player models, animations, and environmental assets
- **Audio:** Sound effects for gameplay feedback

## Future Improvements

- Refine player animations and transitions for more responsive, natural movement.
- Improve stadium visuals and add environmental details to increase immersion.
- Explore an extended gameplay sequence featuring a short obstacle course leading into the penalty kick.

## Running the Project

1. Clone this repository.
2. Open the project using Unity **6000.0.58f2** through Unity Hub.
3. Open the main menu scene and press Play.
